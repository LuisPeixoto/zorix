# OrgChart

The AI workforce as an organisation: virtual managers, department leads, virtual employees and the people they answer to.

- Each box is a WorkerCard. Elbow connectors in `line-strong` join a manager to the people and agents below.
- People are dashed cards with round avatars. Every chain ends at a person who approves.
- A manager plans, assigns, monitors and reports within its autonomy level (see AutonomyDial). What it may do and spend only shrinks down the chart, as it does in a run tree.
