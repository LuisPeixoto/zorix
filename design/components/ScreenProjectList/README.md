# Project list

The same tasks as the board, as a table that can be read and sorted by column.

- Built from PageHeader, Tabs, Chip filters, Table, Badge, Sparkbar and Avatar.
- Columns: key (mono), task, priority (flag, word and tone), assignee (avatar and name), state (Badge), progress (Sparkbar and the number), due date. A late date turns `err` and says "Today" or "2 days late".
- *Needs you* and "Atlas is planning" sit beside the title, as on the board. A row opens the task in a Drawer.
- The filters are the board's: Everyone, Agents, People and Urgent and high. *People* shows what a person has; until accounts exist that is you.
