# MetricTile

A live number with its trend: label, value, change, a detail line and a sparkline.

- The sparkline is an area at 14% over a 1.75px line in the metric's series colour, scaled to the metric's own range.
- The change reads in words and arrows (▲ 12%), toned by whether it is good, not by direction: more spend is `warn`, fewer waiting approvals is `ok`.
- Four to a row at the top of a board, when those figures are the point.
